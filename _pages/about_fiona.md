I am a Geography undergraduate at the University of Cambridge, driven by a deep fascination with climate science and the complex interplay between the cryosphere, atmosphere, and ocean, especially during abrupt climate transitions. My research employs both proxy records and numerical models to reconstruct past climates (e.g. Dansgaard–Oeschger and Heinrich events) as a means to illuminate future thresholds. 

Beyond my formal coursework, I continually teach myself new coding techniques and develop my own `climate models`, and I enjoy sharing insights into interesting scientific questions via `blog posts`. Also, as a former member of my country’s IESO national team, I now help organize and tutor for earth-science Olympiads, and contribute to other open-source geoscience `education materials`.
